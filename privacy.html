Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Compliant with Google Play Developer Policies & Apple App Store Guidelines

Commitment to User Transparency & Accurate Representation

This Privacy Policy clearly describes what data the aayshamaagoatfarm mobile application and website collect, why we need it, how it is handled, and your right to delete your personal data at any time.

1. Who We Are

aayshamaagoatfarm is an agricultural livestock e-commerce platform based in Tamil Nadu, India. Our mobile app enables verified customers to book farm-fresh raw goat meat, free-range country chicken, and meat cuts from Monday through Friday for scheduled refrigerated delivery every Saturday and Sunday.

For questions regarding this policy or our data practices, reach us at: aayishammagoatfarm2027gurbani@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information strictly required to authenticate your profile and deliver perishable meat orders:

  • Personal Identity Information: Name, mobile phone number, and email address for account creation, login OTP authentication, and customer service.
  • Delivery & Location Information: Physical delivery address, pincode, flat/door number, and optional GPS coordinates provided explicitly by you to route weekend meat deliveries to your door.
  • Payment Transaction Data: When placing orders, payment processing is handled entirely by authorized, RBI-compliant third-party payment gateways (Razorpay, UPI, Credit/Debit cards). We never store your full card number, CVV, or banking PINs on our servers.
  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, and anonymous crash analytics to ensure app stability and prevent functionality failures.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely for genuine, transparent purposes:

  • To fulfill, prepare, and deliver weekend meat orders accurately.
  • To send order confirmation, preparation status, and delivery dispatch SMS / WhatsApp updates.
  • To compute and process eligible cancellation refunds according to our published Return and Refund Policy.
  • To prevent fraud, multiple accounts abuse, and bot attacks.

4. Third-Party Services & Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any marketing agencies or third parties. We share limited necessary data only with:

  • Payment Gateways: Secure tokenized payment communication to process orders and refunds.
  • Delivery Personnel: Your delivery address and phone number are shared with designated delivery couriers strictly during active weekend dispatch windows.
  • SMS / OTP Gateways: To transmit login OTPs and order status notifications.

5. Data Security & Retention

All communication between the mobile app, website, and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). User data is retained only for the duration of active account membership or as required by Indian accounting and taxation regulations.

6. User Rights & Complete Account / Data Deletion

In full compliance with Google Play Store and Apple App Store User Data & Account Deletion policies, you have complete control over your account:

  • In-App Deletion: You can initiate complete account and data deletion directly inside the mobile app by navigating to: Profile → Account Settings → Delete Account.
  • Online Deletion Request: You can request immediate deletion of your profile, address book, and contact history by emailing aayishammagoatfarm2027gurbani@gmail.com with your registered mobile number. Requests are fulfilled within 48 to 72 hours.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app and services are directed at adult consumers capable of placing commercial food orders. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18 years of age.

8. Policy Updates & Contact

We may periodically update this policy to reflect operational or regulatory changes. The latest version will always be accessible at this URL: https://aayshamaagoatfarm.com/privacy.html.
