Return and Refund Policy

Last Updated: March 2025 • Applicable to all Mobile App & Website Orders

Perishable Agricultural Goods Notice

Due to the consumable and highly perishable nature of raw livestock meat (goat meat, mutton, and country chicken), physical returns after delivery are strictly not accepted once the package seal has been inspected and handed over.

1. Weekly Order Booking Window

aayshamaagoatfarm accepts customer orders for raw goat meat and country chicken through our mobile application exclusively from Monday 00:01 AM to Friday 11:59 PM. All booked orders are harvested and delivered exclusively on the upcoming Saturday and Sunday.

2. Order Cancellation & Tiered Refund Matrix

Because live livestock are specifically allocated, weighed, and queued for humane dressing based on customer bookings, our refund rules are strictly tiered as follows:

Cancellation Timeframe Eligible Refund Deduction / Rationale
Monday to Thursday
(Up to 11:59 PM Thursday) 		100% Refund Nominal third-party platform / payment gateway processing fee only.
Friday
(00:00 AM to 11:59 PM Friday) 		50% Refund 50% of the total order value is retained to offset livestock batch assignment, farm reservation, and logistics prep.
Saturday & Sunday
(Weekend Delivery Days) 		No Refund (0%) Meat has already been freshly dressed, sanitized, packed, and assigned to active delivery couriers.

3. Delivery Discrepancies & Replacement Protocol

While physical return of raw meat is not permitted for hygiene reasons, customers are entitled to an immediate resolution under the following circumstances:

  • Wrong Item Received: If you ordered goat meat and received country chicken, or received incorrect cut weights exceeding our acceptable variance (±50g).
  • Spoilage or Damaged Seal: If the packaging was tampered with during transit or the product does not meet standard sensory freshness upon arrival.
  • Reporting Window: Customers must report the issue within 2 hours of delivery through the app support button or direct phone hotline, providing clear photographs of the sealed packaging and weighing scale reading.
  • Resolution: Upon verification by our support team, either a replacement batch or a full store refund to original payment source will be issued within 3-5 business days.

4. Mode & Timeline of Refund Processing

All approved refunds are credited back to the original method of payment (UPI, Net Banking, Debit/Credit Card) used during the transaction via our secure payment gateway partner. Refunds typically reflect in the user's account within 3 to 7 working days, subject to the customer's banking institution policies.

5. Unsuccessful Delivery Attempts

If the customer is unavailable at the delivery address, does not answer courier calls, or provides an incorrect address during Saturday/Sunday morning delivery, the order cannot be re-routed or redelivered at a later date due to temperature deterioration. In such cases, no refund will be applicable.
